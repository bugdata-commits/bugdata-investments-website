# BugData Growth Sprint

## Technical SEO foundation

- [ ] The public homepage has a unique title, description, canonical URL, Open Graph tags, Twitter card tags, and crawlable LocalBusiness/Organization/Service/FAQ structured data.
- [ ] The repository serves `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, `manifest.webmanifest`, and a real `404.html`.
- [ ] The sitemap lists only real public pages on `https://bugdata.co.zw/`.

## Search-led information architecture

- [ ] The homepage links to focused service pages for AI automation and SEO, and the blog is discoverable through real HTML links rather than only JavaScript modals.
- [ ] Each public service page has a route-specific title, description, H1, useful body copy, CTA, internal links, and service schema.
- [ ] Location content is published only where BugData genuinely serves the area and does not use thin doorway pages.

## Revenue interface

- [ ] The homepage explains the Zimbabwean SME customer, the managed-service outcome, and the primary CTA above the fold.
- [ ] A visitor can submit name, business, city, website, and WhatsApp number through a short lead module that pre-composes a WhatsApp message.
- [ ] Plans and service cards lead to a clear next action and preserve the existing local payment/contact signals.
- [ ] Unsupported or unverifiable performance claims are not presented as proven results.

## Measurement and authority

- [ ] Search Console and analytics event tracking are documented for WhatsApp clicks, calls, lead submissions, plan clicks, and case-study views.
- [ ] The team has a genuine Google Business Profile, consistent NAP details, original proof, and a review-request process.
- [ ] The first 90-day publishing and case-study cadence is captured in `docs/seo-growth-plan.md`.
